Numbers you can trust.

Bank statements print their own proof: a balance after every transaction. The converter checks every extracted row against those printed balances and marks it Verified — and if a scan is unreadable, it flags the row instead of guessing. You see exactly what reconciles before you export anything.

What it does.

100% offline

Runs entirely on your Windows computer. No uploads, no cloud, no account. Built for people who handle other people's financial documents.

Scanned statements too

Built-in OCR reads paper statements that were scanned to PDF — not just digital originals.

Three export formats

CSV for anything, XLSX for Excel, QBO for QuickBooks. Batch-convert a whole folder at once.

Balance verification

Each transaction is reconciled against the statement's printed running balance, so missing or misread rows can't slip through silently.

Many bank layouts

Single-column and two-column statements, sectioned business statements, trailing-minus formats, and more — with layout inference for banks we haven't seen.

Made for bookkeepers

Bookkeepers, accountants, property managers and small business owners who convert statements every month — without sending clients' data to a website.
